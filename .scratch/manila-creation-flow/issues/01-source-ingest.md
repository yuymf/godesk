## Question

Can the validation fixtures be rendered, inspected, hashed, and traced without
mistaking a broken mirror for a valid source?

## Answer

Yes. The accepted 8-page PDF was rendered and every page visually inspected.

boundaries, and the rejected corrupt PDF are recorded in
`sources/manila/PROVENANCE.md`.
