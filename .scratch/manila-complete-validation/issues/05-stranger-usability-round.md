# Stranger usability gate

Type: research
Status: ready-for-human
Blocked by: 04

## Question

Can at least two of three unfamiliar participants independently compile and open
the seeded playable build while understanding the goal, current step, source
authority, conflict, and consequences?

## Acceptance

- Three consented sessions record time, first-click errors, backtracking,
  facilitator rescues, spoken questions, unresolved terms, consequence
  predictions, completion, and post-session confidence.
- All participants can state the goal and current step.
- At least two resolve the seeded source conflict and compile/open without
  rescue.
- No participant mistakes AI output for an authoritative rule.
- No participant silently passes a blocking unresolved rule.
- Findings become ranked evidence-backed issues.

## Comments

- 2026-07-23: The deterministic exercise, facilitator protocol, and session
  template are ready. No stranger session has been claimed or fabricated.
