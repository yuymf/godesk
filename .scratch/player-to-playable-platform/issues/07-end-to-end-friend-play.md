# End-to-end friend play

Type: task
Status: ready-for-human
Blocked by: 06

## Question

Can an unfamiliar player upload their own rules and assets, generate a game,
invite friends, and complete play without implementation-team help?

## Acceptance

- At least three stranger-led generation sessions complete.
- At least two real friend groups finish a room session.
- Clarification count, generation time, rescues, rule-helper questions,
  disconnects, and completion are recorded.
- Failures become ranked issues; automation is not substituted for human play.
